Rotating mark — pick one

Four takes on the constellation for the hero. All keep the gold sparkle in the middle (no "Sentium Core" text), orbit at a similar cadence, and use the existing brand palette. They differ in density, labels, and how loud the "your business joins us" story is.

V1 · Faithful (local planet orbits)

Each dot revolves around its own label

Constellation as a whole is static - the sparkle, connectors, rings, and labels don't move. What DOES move: each product's dot slowly circles its own label at a fixed radius, each at its own speed (18-26s per lap). Feels like five little product planets each quietly orbiting the name of their star.

This is the one you picked, this time with local orbital motion only.

V2 · Minimal

Dots only, no labels, no connectors

Two concentric rings of brand-coloured dots orbiting the sparkle - each dot subtly stands for a product but never tells you which. Reads as pure "brand mark in motion", not as a product diagram. Closest in spirit to the current site's constellation.

Best if you want the mark quietly on-brand, no explanation required.

V3 · Dense

All six Sentium products + your business

Splits Alfie into Puzzles and Colouring and brings KidWise into the picture, so every shipped product has a star. "Your business" hangs off the outer orbit as an outlier waiting to be pulled in. Denser but more honest about the portfolio.

Best if you want to show the depth of the studio at a glance.

V4 · Brand fusion

Existing constellation + product stars orbiting further out

Keeps the current 16-dot Sentium mark alive (a ring of brand-coloured dots rotating around the sparkle), then adds product stars in a wider orbit with small caps labels. Most continuous with what's already there, adds the new "product constellation" idea as an outer layer.

Best if you don't want to lose brand recognition from the current mark.
