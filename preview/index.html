About

A focused studio for organisations who want it done right.

Sentium is a UK technology studio building consumer products and AI-native systems for organisations that take their software seriously. We design and ship end-to-end - product strategy, engineering, content - with deep cloud expertise, privacy-first architecture, and a bias toward shipping what actually works.

Built by an ex-Microsoft Cloud Solution Architect, we bring the engineering rigour of enterprise to teams that need to move at startup speed. Azure-native by default, cloud-agnostic by capability. We partner with founders, product teams, and operators who want a studio that ships - not a consultancy that produces decks.

What we focus on

Four areas, one bar.

We pick the work for the same reason we'd pick anything else: it's worth the time, it's a real problem, and we'd be a little embarrassed to ship something half-hearted.

Education

Finance

Software for the day-to-day of money - the bills, the goals, the bits everyone keeps meaning to sort out - without making it feel like homework.
